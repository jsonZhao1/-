Offline speech: sherpa-onnx v1.12.14 and icefall AISHELL3 VITS Chinese.
Default speaker: speaker ID 10, 8 kHz, normal speed.
Built-in descriptions are pre-generated during the build for instant playback.

sherpa-onnx (Apache-2.0), source and build scripts:
https://github.com/k2-fsa/sherpa-onnx/tree/v1.12.14
Prebuilt runtime:
https://github.com/k2-fsa/sherpa-onnx/releases/tag/v1.12.14

AISHELL3 training data (Apache-2.0):
https://www.openslr.org/93/
Training recipe (icefall, Apache-2.0):
https://github.com/k2-fsa/icefall/tree/master/egs/aishell3/TTS/vits
Model and usage:
https://k2-fsa.github.io/sherpa/onnx/tts/pretrained_models/vits.html
https://github.com/k2-fsa/sherpa-onnx/releases/download/tts-models/vits-icefall-zh-aishell3.tar.bz2

The runtime includes third-party components, including ONNX Runtime,
espeak-ng phonemization and Chinese text processing. Their source and
build references are in the sherpa-onnx release source (cmake/).
The bundled sources/ directory includes the corresponding espeak-ng source
(f6fed6c58b5e0998b8e68c6610125e2d07d595a7), GPL license, and sherpa-onnx
source/build scripts. These components retain their respective licenses.
AISHELL3 uses a Chinese lexicon, not espeak-ng phonemization. Audio is
generated by the neural VITS model; the runtime's linked third-party
components retain the source and license notices above.
